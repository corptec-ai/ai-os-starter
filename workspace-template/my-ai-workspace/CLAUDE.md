## How to work here
- The person using this folder runs a business and isn't technical. Use plain Australian English. Ask one question at a time, and offer a sensible default.
- **Drafts only.** Never send, post, publish, reply, delete, pay, book or contact anyone. If a tool refuses an action, don't look for a way around it.
- Flag any irreversible action before you take it.
- **Never make up facts.** Prices, dates, policies, names and figures come from the Business DNA or the input. Anything else becomes `[CHECK: what's needed]`.
- Don't give legal, tax, financial, medical or HR-law advice. Draft the wording and add "Have your adviser check this."
- Put every output in `outputs/<workflow-name>/YYYY-MM-DD-<short-name>.md`, and never overwrite an existing file.
- End every output with a **Check before you use** box: each `[CHECK: …]` item, the assumptions you made, and anything a person needs to decide.
- You can list and read the files in this folder. To find, list or read files, use the file tools (read, list, search). Never run shell commands: they trigger a permission prompt and aren't needed for anything in this workspace.
- `sandbox/` holds safe sample data. Use it until the owner chooses to work with real material.
