# Process notes — swapping a gas storage hot water unit (voice note, transcribed)

Recorded by Marco (Senior Plumber) in the van, Thursday 29/10/2026, for the new apprentices. Transcribed as spoken.

---

Righto, this is how we do a straight swap on a gas storage hot water unit, like-for-like, outdoor or cupboard install. Most of these are 135 or 170 litre.

So first thing, when you get there, introduce yourself, ask the customer where the unit is and whether they've noticed anything else, leaks, smells, whatever. Check the new unit on the truck is the right size and the right gas type before you unload it. Natural gas, not LPG. You'd be amazed.

Then you turn the water off at the isolation valve on the cold inlet, or at the meter if the valve's seized, and open a hot tap in the house so it's not under pressure. Hook the hose on the drain valve and run it to the garden or the gutter. Careful, the water in there can still be hot as anything if it's only just died.

While it's draining you can undo the flue and get the old flue cowl off. Then undo the cold, the hot and the relief valve drain line. Oh, and the gas union, obviously — the gas has been off at the meter since before you started on the flue, you don't touch any of the fittings till that's done. Cap the gas line off once it's disconnected.

Walk the old unit out on the trolley. Two people if it's a 170. Check the base or the slab, clean it up, make sure it's level.

New unit in, level it, connect the cold and hot with new flexis or copper depending on what's there, new tempering valve if the old one's past it — most are. Relief valve drain to a proper drain point. Put the flue on. Then the gas connection.

Fill it up before you light it. Open the cold valve, leave a hot tap running inside till you get steady water with no air. Never light it empty.

Then gas back on, spray the joints with the leak detector, look for bubbles. Light the pilot, follow the sticker on the unit. Check the flame, check the flue's drawing. Run a hot tap and check the temp at the tap with the thermometer. 50 degrees max at the bathroom.

Clean up — all packaging, offcuts, old flexis go in the truck. Customer gets shown how to relight the pilot. Take photos of the install, the data plate and the gas connection for the job file.

Back at the office the paperwork goes to Priya the same day so the compliance certificate goes out within two days. Old unit goes to the scrap metal bin at the yard.

That's about it. Usually half a day if nothing's seized.
