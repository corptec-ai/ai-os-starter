# Social post ideas — Northside Plumbing & Gas (sample)

Ideas jotted down by Priya for the business's Facebook and LinkedIn pages, November 2026.

1. **Before you go away for summer.** A quick tip for homeowners: check your hot water unit's relief valve and look for rust or water pooling at the base before the Christmas break, so you don't come home to a cold shower.

2. **Landlords and gas certificates.** Explain in plain language what a gas compliance certificate is, why landlords and property managers should keep a copy on file, and that we send ours within 2 days of every gas job.

3. **Helen Nakamura's new hot water.** We replaced Helen Nakamura's 20-year-old gas unit in Thornbury last week, same day she called. She was so happy she made the boys a cuppa. Put up the photo of her standing next to the new unit at her front gate.

4. **Since 1998.** We've been looking after Melbourne's north for 28 years. A short post about being local, licensed for gas and turning up when we say.
