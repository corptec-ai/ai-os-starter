# Products and services — Northside Plumbing & Gas (example)

## Our offers
- **Hot water replacement:** supply and install gas or electric storage and continuous flow. Includes removal of the old unit. Excludes electrical upgrades. Usually 1 day.
- **Leak detection and repair:** includes diagnosis and standard repair. Excludes wall or tile reinstatement. Same or next day.
- **Commercial gas and grease trap work:** installs, servicing, compliance certificates. Quoted per job.
- **Real estate maintenance:** priority call-outs for agency-managed rentals.

## What we never do
Unlicensed or uncertified gas work. Jobs without a compliance certificate where one is required.
