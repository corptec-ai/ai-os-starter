# Ideal customers — Northside Plumbing & Gas (example)

## Best customers
1. Homeowners in Melbourne's north (Reservoir, Preston, Thornbury, Bundoora) replacing hot water systems or fixing leaks
2. Small commercial kitchens and cafés needing grease traps, gas fit-outs and compliance work
3. Local real estate agencies managing rentals (repeat maintenance)

## Their problems in their words
"My hot water's gone cold." "We've got an inspection next week." "Tenant says there's a leak and I need someone today."

## Why they choose us
Upfront prices, turn up when we say, licensed for gas, local.

## Who we are not for
Large new-build developments; customers who want the cheapest quote and no paperwork.

## Red flags in an enquiry
"Cash job, no invoice." "Can you just bypass the compliance certificate?" Jobs outside our service area (more than 25 km from Reservoir).
