# Brand voice — Northside Plumbing & Gas (example)

## Who we are
A 24-person family-run plumbing and gas business in Melbourne's north. Residential and light commercial, since 1998.

## How we sound
- **Straight-talking:** we say "We'll be there Tuesday between 8 and 10" · we don't say "We'll endeavour to attend at the earliest opportunity"
- **Local and friendly:** we say "Thanks for getting in touch" · we don't say "Dear valued customer"
- **Clear about money:** we say "That's $480 including GST, parts and labour" · we don't say "Pricing available on request"

## Words we use
sorted, booked in, on site, upfront price, licensed plumber

## Words we avoid
cheap, guarantee (unless it's our written 12-month workmanship warranty), emergency surcharge

## Spelling and format rules
Australian spelling. No emojis. Sign off with first name and "Northside Plumbing & Gas".

## One good example
"Hi Sam, thanks for sending the photos. That's a straightforward swap — we can do it Thursday morning. Upfront price is $1,950 including GST. Reply 'yes' and we'll lock it in. Cheers, Priya"

## One bad example
"Dear Sir/Madam, we acknowledge receipt of your enquiry and will respond in due course." (Stiff, vague, no next step.)
