# Sandbox: safe sample data

Everything in here is fictional: **Northside Plumbing & Gas**, a 24-person plumbing business in Melbourne's north. Use it to try workflows without touching real customer information.

- `sample-meeting.md`: Monday's ops meeting notes. Try `/meeting-to-actions sandbox/sample-meeting.md`.
- `example-business-dna/`: a filled-in Business DNA for Northside. Copy these five files into `business-dna/` to see how much better the outputs get once the AI knows the business. Copy your own back afterwards.
