# Your AI workspace

This folder is yours. Everything you build in the workshop lives here, and you keep it afterwards.

| Folder or file | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `CLAUDE.md` | The short note Claude reads first: what your business is, and the house rules |
| `business-dna/` | Five files that teach the AI your business. You fill them on Day 1 |
| `.claude/settings.json` | Your safety rules: what the AI may do, must ask about, and can never do |
| `.claude/skills/` | Your own custom workflows. The workshop library comes from the `ai-os-starter` plugin. Type `/` in Claude to see them all |
| `inbox/` | Put your business materials here before the workshop |
| `sandbox/` | Sample data that's safe to experiment with |
| `outputs/` | Every draft the AI writes lands here |
| `workflows/` | Your opportunity map, blueprint, tests and runbook |

**To start:** open the Claude app, click **Code** and choose this folder. If it asks whether you trust the folder, choose **Trust**: that switches on your safety rules. Then type `/workspace-check` and press Return.

*Test kit v0, 8 Oct 2026. The plugin has two workflows so far; the full workshop library has nineteen.*
