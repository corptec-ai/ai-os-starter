## Input (pasted role notes, two roles)

```
1. Apprentice plumber. Full time, Mon-Fri 7am-3:30pm, based at our Preston depot, out on jobs across the northern suburbs.
Duties: help the plumbers on residential jobs, carry gear, dig trenches, clean up sites, learn hot water and gas installs, keep the van stocked.
Must have: driver's licence (P plates ok), happy working outdoors, reliable.
Nice: some hand tool experience, Cert II pre-apprenticeship.
Young team, would suit a school leaver. Do a one-day unpaid trial first.
Apply to jobs@example.com

2. Office admin. Part time, 3 days a week (Tue-Thu), 9am-3pm, Preston office.
Duties: answer phones, book jobs into the calendar, send quotes the office manager has approved, chase paperwork from the plumbers, keep supplier invoices filed.
Must have: good phone manner, confident with a calendar and email, organised.
Nice: experience in a trades office.
Apply to jobs@example.com
```

## Questions asked (one at a time, each covering both roles)

1. "What's the pay or pay range for each role? (Default: leave it blank for your payroll adviser to fill in.)" → owner pressed Return.
2. Employment type was already in the notes for both roles, so not asked.
3. "When would you like them to start? (Default: as soon as possible)" → "Apprentice in January, admin as soon as possible".
